# Company handbook (benchmark fixture)

Vacation: everyone has 30 days of paid vacation per year. Book vacation in the HR system at least two weeks ahead.

Expenses: submit receipts within 30 days of purchase. Expenses over 5 000 SEK need your manager's approval.

Remote work: you may work remotely up to three days per week. Core hours are 10:00–15:00 CET.

Equipment: new employees get a laptop and a monitor. Report broken equipment to it@company.example.
